Boundary: arithmetic only Shim Math adds shim thicknesses from the numbers you type. The gap and every thickness are yours to measure or read off your own job. It treats every shim as flat and exact, so it has not seen your job and does not know the parts' flatness or wear, how shims compress under load, heat, or what gap is right for your machine, and it gives no verdict that a fit, an assembly or a machine is adequate or safe. Every number is your own entry.
