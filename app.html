Shim Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page adds shim thicknesses from the numbers you type. The gap and every thickness are yours to measure or read off your own job. It treats every shim as flat and exact, so it has not seen your job and does not know the parts' flatness or wear, how shims compress under load, heat, or what gap is right for your machine, and it gives no verdict that a fit, an assembly or a machine is adequate or safe. Every number is your own entry.

The stack truth

What your picked shims add up to against the gap. Gap in mm, stack as thicknesses in mm separated by commas.

The pick truth

The fewest shims from your kit that fill the gap, or the closest they reach.

The step truth

The finest step your kit can hit exactly, and the nearest exact hits around the gap.

Shim Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
